# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its Code of Conduct. The full text is canonical and is reproduced
below in summarised form for convenience.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or
invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open,
welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behaviour

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility and apologising to those affected by mistakes
- Focusing on what is best for the overall community

## Unacceptable behaviour

- The use of sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or
  advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political
  attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour
may be reported to the project maintainer at
**ceyhun.olcan.27@dartmouth.edu**. All reports will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly. The privacy and security of the
reporter will be respected.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (the GitHub
repository, issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, and any
official communication channels) and when an individual is officially
representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement guidelines

The maintainer will follow the Contributor Covenant Community Impact
Guidelines in determining consequences for violations:

1. **Correction**: Private written warning for inappropriate language or
   behaviour deemed unprofessional or unwelcome.
2. **Warning**: Written warning with consequences for continued behaviour
   for a violation through a single incident or series of actions.
3. **Temporary ban**: Temporary ban from any sort of interaction or
   public communication with the community for a specified period for a
   serious violation of community standards.
4. **Permanent ban**: Permanent ban from any sort of public interaction
   within the community for a pattern of violation or for harassment of
   an individual.
